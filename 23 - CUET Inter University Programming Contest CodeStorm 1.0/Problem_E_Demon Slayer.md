# Demon Slayer

**URL:** https://toph.co/p/demon-slayer  

---

Tanjiro is a member of the Demon Slayer Corps whose job is to kill demons. In the epic Demon vs Human war, Tanjiro is facing

$n$

demons. The

$i^{th}$

$(1≤i≤n)$

demon has a power of

$a_i$

$(a_1,a_2,....,a_n)$

. As a human, Tanjiro also has a health

$h$

. After killing the

$i^{th}$

demon, his health decreases by

$a_i$

. Now, Tanjiro wants to kill as many demons as possible while he is alive. What is the maximum number of demons he can kill before dying as a hero.

Input

The first line of the input contains

$n$

$(1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5)-$

the number of demons and

$h$

$(0 ≤ h ≤ 10^9)-$

the initial health of Tanjiro. The next line contains

$n$

space-separated integers

$a_i$

$(1 ≤ a_i ≤ 10^9)$

describing the power of the

$i^{th}$

$(1 ≤ i ≤ n)$

demon.

Output

Output the maximum number of demons Tanjiro can kill.

Examples
